The short version

Your trips stay on your device. The calculator does not upload them, does not use cookies, and does not track you.

What is saved, and where

The calculator saves a few things in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time:

  • Your trips: country, entry date, and departure date
  • Your appearance choices: Auto, Light, or Dark mode, and the color scheme
  • Whether you closed the Add to Home Screen banner

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or passport details
  • No cookies
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools

Files you download or share

Exported records (CSV, JSON, TXT) and map images (JPG, PNG, SVG) are created on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. When you use Share, only what you choose to share is sent, and it goes to the app or website you pick. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

Services the page relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives standard connection details such as your IP address when you load the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain name service (DNS) that points your browser to the site. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Neither service receives your trips. The page's typeface is stored on this site, so loading it sends nothing to Google or any other font service.

Changes

If the calculator ever starts collecting anything, such as anonymous visit counts, this page will be updated first and will explain what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through All Trips Considered.
